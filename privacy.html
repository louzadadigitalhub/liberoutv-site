Privacy Policy

Applies equally to the LIBEROU TV apps on Samsung, LG, Roku and Apple devices · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the same player in these official stores:
  • Samsung Smart TV — Samsung Apps
  • LG Smart TV — LG Content Store
  • Roku — Roku Channel Store
  • iPhone, iPad and Apple TV — App Store
  • Mac — App Store and the installer downloaded from this site

The rules, the data and the contact are the same on every platform. There is no separate policy per store.

This policy explains what data the LIBEROU TV applications collect, why they collect it, and who it is shared with.

Who we are

LIBEROU TV. Privacy contact: liberoutv@pm.me.

What the application collects

On each session, and about every 15 minutes while it is open, the application sends the following data to our configuration server:

Samsung: DUID

Roku: channel client ID
iPhone, iPad and Apple TV: identifier for vendor (identifierForVendor), which is not the advertising identifier
Mac: an identifier created by the app itself at installation

Streaming server address in useTechnical support and connection diagnosis.
IP addressRecorded by the server when it receives the connection, as with any internet request.

Who we share it with

When you sign in with a provider code, that provider, which is the service you subscribe to, can see the data above for the devices using its code: username, device, version, country and last activity. It uses them for support. No other third party receives this data.

To show the match schedule, the application queries an external sports results service. That service receives the device's IP address, as with any internet request, and does not receive any data from your account.

What the application does NOT collect

  • We do not collect your civil name, home address, tax ID, e-mail or phone number through the application.
  • We do not use advertising or ad networks.
  • We do not use third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not access the microphone, camera, contacts, files or precise location.
  • We do not sell, rent or hand data to third parties for marketing.

Your password

The password of your subscription is stored only in the device’s local storage (on Apple devices, in the system Keychain), so you do not have to type it every time. It is not sent to our configuration server.

To play content, the application sends username and password to the streaming server of your subscription, which authenticates access and delivers the catalog. That traffic goes directly from the device to that server.

When you sign out from Profile, the data saved on the device is deleted. This is the same on Samsung, LG, Roku and Apple devices.

Content

The application is a media player. It does not host or distribute its own content: it only plays the catalog of the service you already subscribe to, using the credentials you enter. Without a valid login, the application shows no content. This is the same on every platform listed above.

How long we keep it

Session records are kept while your subscription is active and for up to 12 months afterwards, for support and security. They are then deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, and you may withdraw consent. Write to liberoutv@pm.me and we will reply within 15 days.

This website

The website app.liberoutv.com does not use cookies, advertising or tracking tools. It stores two preferences in your browser, the language and the home page animation, and they never leave your device. The site's server records the IP address and the page requested, as with any internet request.

Children

The application is rated 16+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page, with the update date at the top. The update applies to Samsung, LG, Roku and Apple together.
