Privacy Policy

Applies equally to the LIBEROU PLAYER apps on Samsung, LG, Roku, Apple, Android and Amazon Fire TV devices · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the same player in these official stores:
  • Samsung Smart TV — Samsung Apps
  • LG Smart TV — LG Content Store
  • Roku — Roku Channel Store
  • Amazon Fire TV — Amazon Appstore
  • Android (phone, tablet, Android TV, Google TV and TV box) — Google Play
  • Android — installer downloaded from this website
  • iPhone, iPad and Apple TV — App Store
  • Mac — App Store and the installer downloaded from this site

The rules, the data and the contact are the same on every platform. There is no separate policy per store.

This policy explains what data the LIBEROU PLAYER applications collect, why they collect it, and who it is shared with.

Who we are

LIBEROU PLAYER is an application by LOUZADA DIGITAL HUB, which is responsible for the processing of the data described here. Privacy contact: louzadadigitalhub@gmail.com.

Who we share it with

When you sign in with a provider code, that provider, which is the service you subscribe to, can see the data above for the devices using its code: username, device model, platform, version, country and last activity. It uses them for support. No other third party receives this data.

Channel logos and movie posters are downloaded from the addresses given by your provider's server; those servers receive the device's IP address. The Trailer button opens the YouTube app.

To show the match schedule, the application queries our server (in older versions of some apps, an external sports results service). Whoever answers receives the device's IP address, as with any internet request, and does not receive any data from your account.

What the application does NOT collect

  • We do not collect your civil name, home address, tax ID, e-mail or phone number through the application.
  • We do not use advertising or ad networks.
  • We do not use third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not access the microphone, camera, contacts, files or precise location.
  • We do not sell, rent or hand data to third parties for marketing.

Your password

The password of your subscription is stored only in the device’s local storage (on Apple devices, in the system Keychain; on Android, in the app’s private storage), so you do not have to type it every time. It is not sent to our configuration server. On Android, the password is not included in the system backup.

To play content, the application sends username and password to the streaming server of your subscription, which authenticates access and delivers the catalog. That traffic goes directly from the device to that server. The connection to that server may not be encrypted (http): that depends on the provider, not on the application.

When you sign out from Profile, the data saved on the device is deleted. This is the same on Samsung, LG, Roku, Apple and Amazon Fire TV devices.

On Android, signing out deletes the saved sign-in. With “Remember my details” checked, the code, username and password stay saved to fill in the sign-in screen; unchecking it deletes them. Channel and movie lists, favorites and “continue watching” stay on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings.

Content

The application is a media player. It does not host or distribute its own content: it only plays the catalog of the service you already subscribe to, using the credentials you enter. Without a valid login, the application shows no content. This is the same on every platform listed above.

How long we keep it

The data of each device is kept while it uses the application and deleted 12 months after its last use. The usage record of each username is deleted 35 days after its last use. Daily totals, with no one’s name, are kept for billing providers.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, and you may withdraw consent. Write to louzadadigitalhub@gmail.com and we will reply within 15 days.

How to delete your data (LIBEROU PLAYER, by LOUZADA DIGITAL HUB)

Write to louzadadigitalhub@gmail.com with the provider code and your username. We delete the records of that username (the devices, with model, system, country and app version, the days of use and the usage record) within 15 days and reply when it is done. Only daily device totals, with no one’s name, are kept, for billing providers.

On the device, you can delete them yourself: sign out from Profile, uncheck “Remember my details” (on Android) or uninstall the application.

This website

The website liberouplayer.com does not use cookies, advertising or tracking tools. It stores two preferences in your browser, the language and the home page animation, and they never leave your device. The site's server records the IP address and the page requested, as with any internet request.

Children

The application is rated 16+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page, with the update date at the top. The update applies to Samsung, LG, Roku, Apple, Android and Amazon Fire TV together.
