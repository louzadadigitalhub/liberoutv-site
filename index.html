Questions

Does LiberouTV come with channels or movies?
No. It is only the player. What you see on screen comes from the service you subscribe to.
Do I pay for the app?
No. The app is free and has no purchases inside it. The subscription is contracted with your provider.
Where do I get my sign-in details?
From your provider, who looks after your subscription.
Does it work on my device?
The app is already available for Windows, Mac and Android, on the downloads page. The other platforms in the list above are on the way.
What do you keep about me?
The minimum for the app to work. It is all in the privacy policy.
